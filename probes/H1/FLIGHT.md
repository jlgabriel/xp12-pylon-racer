# Probe H1: our pylons, in a pack of our own

**The question:** a DSF written by XOP's code, with an `OBJECT_DEF` **relative to the pack** and
OBJ8 + PNG objects generated by the app: does it load and look as it should?

XOP only wrote library paths (`lib/…`). The pack-relative syntax exists (the Paris overlay that XOP
took apart uses it, `reference/dsf-overlay.md`), but none of our files has flown it yet.

## The design

`make.ts` generates `XPR_H1_Pylons/`: three pylons (green, red and orange, with white stripes) that
are **20 m tall and 2.4 m wide at the base**, and a 534-byte DSF with six pylons in three 40 m gates
on **SCEL's runway 17L**, at 1500, 2000 and 2500 m from the threshold (runway end coordinates taken
from the 12.4.3 `apt.dat`).

- **Positive control:** a stock-library control tower, 150 m east of the green pair. It already
  loaded in XOP's probes. If the tower is there and the pylons are not, the pack loaded and the
  objects did not.
- **Unmistakable paint:** five stripes, color at the foot and at the tip. Read upside down it looks
  the same, so an inverted texture cannot make the probe fail for the wrong reason (the unit test
  does cover the direction).
- **`ATTR_no_cull`:** which winding X-Plane takes as the front face has not been probed, and
  drawing the back faces of a 16-sided tube costs nothing.
- **No rotation:** a pylon is round.
- DSFTool (Ortho4XP's) decompiles it without errors: 4 `OBJECT_DEF`, 7 `OBJECT`, and each pylon
  within 10 cm of what was asked, inside the DSF's ~21 cm quantization.

## How to read it

| You see | Reading |
|---|---|
| Three pairs of colors, on the pavement, crisp stripes | ✅ the whole pack path works |
| White or gray pylons | the `.obj` loaded and the PNG did not: check `Log.txt` |
| Tower yes, pylons no | the pack-relative `OBJECT_DEF` did not resolve: check `Log.txt` |
| Nothing | the pack did not load (`scenery_packs.ini`) |
| Pylons floating or buried | the mesh under the runway is not what we thought; note by how much |

Also, for the design: **from what distance the pylons can be told apart** in the takeoff race.
Without `ATTR_LOD`, X-Plane decides.

## Result: flown 2026-10-24, X-Plane 12.4.3 ✅

- **The pack loads:** `Log.txt` records `DSF load time … for file Custom Scenery/XPR_H1_Pylons/…`
  without a single object or texture error. **The pack-relative `OBJECT_DEF` works.**
- **They show up, with their paint:** in Juan's screenshots (from the threshold and in the replay)
  the three pairs have crisp stripes in green, red and orange. The cone that narrows toward the top
  looks like a pylon, with no seams or inverted faces at 15–20 m.
- **Resting on the pavement**, with their shadow. They neither float nor sink.
- **Distance:** from the threshold the pairs can already be told apart at 1.5–2.5 km (the screenshot
  shows the green and red ones down the runway).
- The control tower: it did not end up in the screenshots. It was not needed as a control, because
  the pylons themselves loaded.

The same happened with the **Daggett Sprint installed by the app** (`XPR_daggett-sprint`, KDAG): it
was raced in full the same day. The real installer, not only the probe, leaves pylons that X-Plane
loads.
